# Third-party runtime notices

The desktop build uses Electron 45.2.1 for Windows x64.

- Official release: https://github.com/electron/electron/releases/tag/v45.2.1
- Source: https://github.com/electron/electron
- Electron uses the MIT license; bundled Chromium and other components have their own notices.
- Runtime checksums are pinned in desktop/runtime.json.
- The packaged app retains LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html from the official archive.

Runtime binaries and third-party source trees are not committed here. The build downloads the official archive or accepts a matching local archive.
